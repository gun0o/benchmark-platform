### `--pin` on vs off

1 thread. Pinning a single worker sends it to vCPU 0, which also carries the guest's interrupt work.

### trial length

A longer trial averages more jitter inside each measurement, so CoV should fall. It also takes proportionally longer in wall time, so it collects more thermal drift - the two effects pull in opposite directions.

| setting | `cpu_int_ops` | `cpu_fp_ops` | `cpu_hash_ops` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| --trial-ms 10 | 5.41% (5.3-6.4) | 3.69% (3.3-4.4) | 6.16% (5.0-6.2) |
| --trial-ms 50 | 3.90% (3.8-5.3) | 3.80% (3.0-4.0) | 3.96% (3.4-4.7) |
| --trial-ms 200 | 2.83% (2.6-3.4) | 3.01% (2.2-3.1) | 3.23% (2.9-3.8) |
